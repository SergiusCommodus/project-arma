Section V · Method

How prices are set

a.Sold, not asked

Ranges come from completed sales. Asking prices on a rack are what the store hopes to get, so they never count.

b.Median, trimmed

One bidding war can skew an average. Each range drops the top and bottom outliers and reports the median, with the sample size beside it.

c.Condition matters

Good, Very Good, and Excellent are priced separately. A matching, correct rifle and a mixed parts rebuild are different markets.

d.Independent

Stores can sponsor a listing. They cannot buy a better verdict or move a number.
